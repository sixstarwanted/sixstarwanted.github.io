Privacy Policy · Clip Studio

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Clip Studio is a personal desktop tool used by its owner to edit short videos and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram and YouTube). It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Who we are

Clip Studio is operated by Six Star Wanted, an individual content creator based in Italy.
Contact: clipzlabs@gmail.com

What data Clip Studio uses

When the owner connects one of their own accounts, Clip Studio receives from the platform, through its official login (OAuth):

  • an access token that lets it publish videos to that account;
  • basic profile information (username, account ID);
  • statistics about the videos it published (views, likes, comments, shares), where the platform provides them.

Clip Studio does not collect data about other people, followers or viewers. It does not use cookies, advertising or analytics trackers.

How the data is used

Only to upload and publish the owner's own videos, with their title, caption and hashtags, and to show the owner the performance of their own posts.

Where the data is stored

All data, including access tokens, is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not sent to any server operated by Clip Studio (there is none).

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Data is kept for as long as the account stays connected. The owner can delete it at any time by disconnecting the account in Clip Studio, which deletes the stored token, or by revoking access from the platform's settings (TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions).

YouTube API Services

Clip Studio uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
