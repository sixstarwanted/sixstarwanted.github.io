What Clip Studio is

Clip Studio is a personal desktop tool operated by Six Star Wanted to edit short-form videos and publish them to the operator's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts. It is not a public service: it has no registration and no users other than its operator.

Use of platform APIs

Clip Studio uses only the official APIs of TikTok, Instagram (Meta) and YouTube (Google), within their terms and rate limits. Every post is made with the account owner's explicit authorization, through the platform's official login.

Content

The operator publishes only content they own or are authorized to use (for example, through creator clipping campaigns that grant permission to repost and edit the creator's streams). The operator is responsible for following each platform's community guidelines and copyright rules.
